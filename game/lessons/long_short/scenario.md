# Long and short

Goal: long / short, longer / shorter; the method - things compared from ONE line. Age 3-4. Characters: Deer (right),
Fox and Squirrel (left). Stage 1, lesson 4. Thread: a picnic across the brook.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *The brook* (the bridge is far away - a little one of our own). Two planks on the grass; the short one lies further
out. Squirrel: "this one is longer, it reaches further" (the naive try); laid across the water it does not reach and
falls in. Deer: it only lay further out; to compare length, start from one line. **Child** drags both planks to the
pencil line (they snap there), points to the longer; laid across, it reaches; they walk over it into the picture.
B. *The meadow* (wind). Squirrel's scarf is too short; a second one; **child** lays both from the line and points to
the longer; Squirrel puts it on, warm. *Breather:* a snail race - point to a snail to cheer it on. Where is the picnic
glade? Deer's map (`znalezisko` mapa_pikniku): two paths from the same start on the meadow to the blanket, one almost
straight, one winding round a hill; Fox is hungry - **child** points to the shorter path; Deer names both (this one
shorter, that one longer) and they take the short one.
C. *The picnic glade* (`polana_piknik`, a place of its own: a checked blanket, a basket, an oak). Squirrel looks round:
the short path got them here in no time. The picnic: one long baguette for everybody.

## Stage 2 - practice (an insert with clouds, five stars)
Ribbons every which way - from the line, then the longer; the shortest of three planks; two pencil snakes; shadows of a
long or a short scarf; catch the long ribbon (twice).

## Stage 3 - at home (the hallway)
The scarves compared from the line; the long one dragged to tall Fox, the short one to small Squirrel. "Compare
shoelaces or spaghetti with your parents - always from one line."

## Iterations
- The naive try is the classic misconception: the end that reaches further is taken for the longer thing; the plank
  falling into the water is its visible consequence, the start line is the method.
- Deer is a quadruped and is never dressed, so the scarf goes to Squirrel (the user's choice).
- Every comparison has the same thing at two lengths (one picture sheet, one scale), never two different things.
