# In front and behind - hide-and-seek

Goal: in front of / behind - behind a thing you are hidden and only a bit peeks out; in front of it everybody sees you.
Age 3-4. Characters: Hedgehog, Bunny, Bear. Stage 5, lesson 18.

**Misconception:** hiding = not seeing - Bunny stands in front of the bush with his eyes shut: "nobody can see me!".

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Forest.* Hide-and-seek; Bear counts to five. Bunny "hides" in front of the bush - Bear sees him at once; Hedgehog:
in front everybody sees you, hide behind. **Child** hides the mouse behind the bush (`place` "za": her ears peek
out). Then the child seeks: a bush, a stump and a stone - Bunny's ears and the duckling's head peek out from behind
two of them; **child** points to each hiding place; the one found jumps out.
B. *Meadow.* "In front - behind": **child** puts the teddy in front of the house (`place` "przed", new relation), then
behind it; then points to what is in front of the house (a ball) and who hides behind it (a hen).

C. *Meadow, then the garden maze* (new scenery `ogrod_labirynt`). The mouse hid again and Bear cannot find her. Bunny
took a photo while everyone hid (`znalezisko` zdjecie_chowany, flies up from Bunny): **child** points to where the mouse
is - only her ears peek out from BEHIND the hedge, while the hen stands IN FRONT of the bench, seen whole. Bunny knows
the hedge: the garden with the maze. Arrival, a look around; Bear is too big for the maze, Hedgehog is small and walks in
through the arch; **child** leads him through the hedge maze (`labirynt` labirynt_chowany, a breather without maths) to
the mouse. Found: she was behind the hedge, so nobody saw her. Hedgehog: hide behind something - in front everybody
sees you.

## Stage 2 - practice
The mouse behind the box; the duckling in front of the basket; whose ears peek out from behind the bush; what is in
front of the armchair; the bunny behind the big stone.

## Stage 3 - at home (room)
The teddy hides behind the curtain (his paws peek out under it) - find him; the ball in front of the armchair.
"Hide-and-seek with your parents - and remember: in front, everybody sees you."
