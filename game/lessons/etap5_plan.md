## 18. In front and behind (new)

**Idea:** in front of / behind - behind a thing you are hidden (only a bit peeks out), in front of it you are seen;
in front of a character is where it looks, behind is its back.
**Misconception:** hiding = not seeing - Bunny stands IN FRONT of the bush with his paws over his eyes: "you can't see
me!". Hedgehog: you're in front of the bush, everybody sees you; hide BEHIND it.

- **Story.** Hide-and-seek in the forest; Bear is the seeker.
  1. *Forest.* Bear counts to five, eyes shut. Bunny "hides" in front of the bush, paws over his eyes - everybody
     laughs. **Child** hides Bunny (his sticker) behind the bush (`place` "za": only his ears peek out).
  2. Now the child seeks: friends hide behind a stump, a bush and a stone - only an ear, a tail, a paw peeks out.
     "Who is behind the stump?" (point to the right hiding place; the one found jumps out).
  3. *Helping.* Bear lost his backpack - it hangs on his back: Bear turns round (the back view) - behind him!
  4. *Breather:* "in front - behind" game: Hedgehog says "in front of the tree!", "behind the tree!" - the child
     drags the hedgehog sticker there (in front = the NEW relation, see below).
  5. Everybody found; the winner hides next.
- **Practice:** hide the mouse behind the box; put the duck in front of the basket; whose ears peek out from behind
  the bush; what is in front of the house; in front or behind (two pictures).
- **Home:** hide-and-seek at home: the teddy behind the curtain, the ball in front of the bed. "Play hide-and-seek
  with your parents: behind the door, behind the armchair."

## 20. The queue (rethought)

The old lesson (Deer's lemonade stand, Fox and Deer only) is "point to the first / last / third" in a row. Rethought
around a race and a podium - ordinals come from WHEN you arrive, not from size.
**Idea:** first, second, third ... last - counted from the front (the finish, the head of a queue); ordinals say
place in order, not size.
**Misconception:** the biggest is first - Mole (who sees badly) says Bunny won "because he's the biggest", but the
little hedgehog crossed the line first.

- **Story.** The great forest race; the prize - medals.
  1. *Meadow.* Animals line up at the start; the race runs (stickers move at different speeds) to a ribbon finish.
     Mole: "Bunny won, he's the biggest!" Owl: first is the one who reached the finish first. **Child** points to the
     first, then the last.
  2. The podium: **child** stands the winners on places 1, 2, 3 (`slots` with numbers).
  3. *Playground.* A queue for the slide: first, second, third (numbers over heads); the first slides down and joins
     the END - who is first now? (the old lesson's good bit, kept).
  4. *Breather:* the friends race (characters walk at different speeds) - who came first, who last.
  5. Medals are given in order - the third medal to the third in the queue.
- **Practice:** point to the second in a queue; who is last; the podium; which place did the duck take (1-3 cards);
  give the medal to the third.
- **Home:** the bathroom queue: brushing teeth in order (Mole first, Beaver second...); "at dinner tell who sat down
  first, second, last".

- A small helper in the lesson (no engine change): a thing hidden behind a scenery sticker with only a part peeking
  out (the existing "behind" placement, lower), jumping out when found.
- Stickers: medals (gold, silver, bronze), a finish ribbon on two posts, a curtain, an armchair, a stone big enough to
  hide behind.
