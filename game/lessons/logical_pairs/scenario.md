# What goes with what - things after the storm

Goal: logical pairs - things that belong together because we use them together, not because they look alike. Age 3-4.
Characters: Beaver, Mole, Deer. Stage 3, lesson 12.

**Misconception:** pairing by look - Mole, who sees badly, pairs a red cup with a red apple.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Brook.* A storm in the night scattered everybody's things. Mole puts the red cup by the red apple - "both red";
Deer: they only share a colour; a pair is two things we use together - the cup goes on its saucer.
B. *Beaver's workshop* (new scenery). **Child** joins three pairs with a finger-drawn line (`polacz`: a brush and paints,
a bee and a flower, a hammer and a nail); a wrong line wobbles off. *Whose?* The pairs go back to their owners - whose
are the hammer and the nail? Mole thinks his, but sees badly and is not sure. Beaver finds an old photo of Mole on the
shelf (a found picture, `znalezisko` "zdjecie_kreta"): **child** points to what Mole holds in his paw - a hammer. So
the hammer is his; and the nail goes with the hammer, because we use them together - so the nail is his too. Mole gets
both ("I'll fix the shelf by my burrow"). *Helping:* Deer's round door has a star keyhole -
which key fits (the star one). *Breather:* catch the last leaves of the storm.

## Stage 2 - practice
Join three pairs; what goes with the bee; memory of pairs (different pictures that belong together); which one does not
belong; join three more.

## Stage 3 - at home (hallway)
Everybody's things back with their pairs (joined by lines). "Play 'what goes with what' with your parents."
