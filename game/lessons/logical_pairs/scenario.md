# What goes with what - things after the storm

Goal: logical pairs - things that belong together because we use them together, not because they look alike. Age 3-4.
Characters: Beaver, Mole, Deer. Stage 3, lesson 12.

**Misconception:** pairing by look - Mole, who sees badly, pairs a red cup with a red apple ("both red"). At home it
comes back as a red hat with a red shoe.

**One thread:** the night's storm scattered everybody's things. Mole's hammer and nail go back to him, Deer gets her
star key back and invites everyone to tea in her house - the home stage is that tea.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Brook.* The friends meet in the morning; Beaver tells of the night - the storm SEEN (event illustration
`burza_noc`: things flying over the brook), Mole hid in his burrow. Deer: look how many things lie by the brook (turned
and scattered). Mole helps: he carries the red cup to the red apple - "both red, so a pair!" Deer (close-up): they only
share a colour, we use them for nothing together. Mole: "then how?" The method on a plain-colour **insert**: a pair is two
things we use together - the spoon goes into the bowl, "we eat soup from the bowl with the spoon; they go together though
they do not look alike". Back at the brook **child** finds what goes with the toothbrush (toothpaste, not the apple or the
flower); Mole names it and gets it (`zarowka`). Beaver: the wind threw the rest through my workshop window.
B. *Beaver's workshop.* Arrival: the workbench is all mud. *Breather* (`zdrapka` "bloto_warsztat"): **child** wipes the
mud off with a finger; brush, paints, hammer, nail, cup and saucer appear, Deer names them. **Child** joins three pairs
with a finger-drawn line (`polacz`: brush - paints, hammer - nail, cup - saucer); Mole names them. *Whose?* the hammer and
the nail - Mole thinks his, but sees badly. Beaver finds Mole's old photo, torn by the storm: **child** puts the torn
picture together (`podarty` "zdjecie_kreta", 4 pieces) and points to what Mole holds - a hammer; the nail goes with the
hammer, so it is his too; Mole gets both. *Helping:* Deer cannot open her door - on an **insert** her star-keyhole door
and three keys: **child** points to the star key; it goes in, rays. Deer: thank you - come to my house for tea!

## Stage 2 - practice
On a plain colour, five stars: join three pairs (spoon - bowl, hen - egg, bee - flower, each named after); what goes with
the pillow (the bed); memory of pairs (named after); which one does not belong (cup, saucer, hammer); each thing waits for
its pair - **drag** the pair into the dashed place beside it (bowl - spoon, shoe - sock, door - key). No bare "Brawo":
every right answer is named.

## Stage 3 - at home: the third act (`przedpokoj`, Deer's hallway)
1. **Arrival with the thread**: Mole: Deer's house! Deer opens the door with the star key, "come in" - the tea.
2. **The misconception again**: the open window let the storm in here too (hat, shoe, sock, scarf, umbrella, boots
   scattered). Mole puts the red hat by the red shoe - "both red!"; Beaver: colour again? a hat is not worn on a foot,
   a sock goes with a shoe.
3. **Interactions, all different**: **child** joins three pairs with lines (shoe - sock, hat - scarf, umbrella - boots);
   **child** drags things onto their painted places (`miejsca`: the hat on the hooks, the boots on the doormat, the shoe
   on the shoe shelf); **child** points to what goes with the teapot (the cup).
4. **The payoff SEEN**: the event illustration `herbatka_u_sarenki` - Deer pours, every cup on its saucer, Mole with his
   hammer under his arm.
5. **The home task shown in play**: "what goes with what" - Deer shows a spoon, Mole finds the bowl; then **child** finds
   what goes with the star key (the door). Deer: play it with your parents - they show a thing, you find its pair at home.

## Iterations
- **v-next, 2026-10-29** (lesson pass): the storm is SEEN (`burza_noc`), not only told; the concept follows the
  guideline steps (Mole's naive pair, Deer's objection, "then how?", the method on an insert with spoon and bowl, the
  child's first pair - the toothbrush); the "catch leaves" breather is gone, the breather is the mud wiped off Beaver's
  workbench (`zdrapka`, new picture `bloto_warsztat`), which also reveals the things to pair; Mole's photo is now torn
  by the storm and put together first (`podarty`); Deer's key on an insert, and her invitation to tea is the thread into
  the home stage. Practice: the bee task became the pillow, the second "join with your finger" became dragging each pair
  into its place (`slots`), the bare "Brawo!" lines became lines naming the pairs, "five games" dropped (no counting past
  three). Home is a third act: the key opens Deer's door, the red hat with the red shoe, pairs joined, things on the
  painted places, the teapot's cup, the tea seen (`herbatka_u_sarenki`), the game played. "... Wskaż." lines made one
  sentence. Editing: dialogues as two-shots, instructions in the task's wide shot, close-ups for reactions only, two
  inserts in the story. New stickers `czajniczek`, `parasol`, `kalosze`, `pasta` (sheet `pary_dom`). The new mechanics
  (siatka, regula ...) were not used: logical pairs are one-to-one links of use, which `polacz`, `slots` and the
  painted places already show; a grid or a hidden sorting rule would teach grouping by feature - the misconception.
