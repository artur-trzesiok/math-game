# The queue - the great forest race

Goal: ordinal numbers - first, second, third ... last - say place in order, counted from the front (the finish, the
head of a queue), not size. Age 4-5. Characters: Mole, Owl, Beaver. Stage 5, lesson 20.

**Misconception:** the biggest is first - Mole: "Bunny will win, he's the biggest!", but the little hedgehog crossed
the line first. At home he says it again of his teddy ("Teddy won, he's the biggest!") and the ball beats it.

**The thread:** medals for the winners. The race on the track, the medals on the podium; Mole wants his toys to have a
race and medals too - the home stage is that race, with paper medals and a ceremony on a podium of books.

No counting past five (numbers_1_5 comes before); place words up to "fourth" and "last".

## Stage 1 - story
A. *The race track* (new panorama `tor_wyscigu`: the start with bunting on the left, the finish flag on the right).
Beaver: the great forest race, medals for the winners. Mole names the runners and is sure: "Bunny is the biggest, he
will win!" **The start** (event illustration `start_wyscigu`): the four at the chalk line, Owl's flag up, Beaver with
his camera; Owl: "Ready, steady, go!". The runners run in lanes and the camera follows them along the track (`pan`),
everybody walking along, to the finish: a close finish. Mole: "Bunny won, he's the biggest!" Owl (a two-shot): in a
race it is not the biggest; first is who reached the finish first, then second, third, and last. Mole did not see who
that was. Beaver took a photo of the finish (`znalezisko` fotofinisz, flies up from Beaver): **child** points to who
touches the line first (the hedgehog - so small, and first; Owl reads the photo: Bunny second, the duckling third), then
**child** points to who came last (the mouse - last, but she finished too). Beaver: the medals are given on the podium;
while it gets ready, the playground.
B. *Playground.* A queue for the slide; Mole: "but who is first here?". **Insert** on a plain colour (the method): the
same queue, a blue arrow at its front, Owl counts from the arrow and the pencil writes 1, 2, 3, 4 over the heads;
**child** points to the third (named: "the mouse first, Bunny second, Hedgehog third"). Back at the slide: the mouse
slides down and goes to the end of the queue, everybody steps forward; **child** points to who is first now (Bunny: when
the first one goes, everybody moves forward). *Helping someone:* the snail comes late and wants to slide too - **child**
drags him to his place; dropped at the front he is pushed back ("no cutting in - who comes last stands at the end"),
at the end he stays: now he is last. *Breather* (`lustro`, oddechy_plan.md): the queue is long and Mole is bored, Owl:
let's pull faces - Mole pulls a face and the **child** points to the same face (three rounds). Beaver: the podium is
ready.
C. *The podium glade* (`podium_wyscigu`: bunting and flowers; the podium is a painted sticker). Arrival, a look around.
**Child** puts the first three on the podium (1 in the middle and highest, 2 left, 3 right; `sort` into the steps).
Owl names the medals: gold for first, silver for second, bronze for third; the gold flies to Hedgehog; **child**
points to who gets the silver (Bunny, named); the bronze goes to the duckling. Confetti; Mole: it was not the biggest
who won, but the fastest - and at home his toys will have a race, with medals too.

## Stage 2 - practice (a plain colour)
The second in a queue; the last in a queue; a race and its podium (the same podium sticker); which medal is for the
second (gold, silver, bronze); the duckling wants to stand third - **child** drags her into the queue between the
second and the third (dropped elsewhere she is pushed back and counted from the arrow). Every right answer is named,
five stars.

## Stage 3 - at home: the third act (`pokoj`, Mole's room)
1. **Arrival with the thread**: Mole: we are home - now the toys' race, and medals after, like in the forest.
2. **The misconception again**: Owl starts the race on the rug: the teddy, the ball and the block run to a pencil
   finish line; Mole: "Teddy won, he's the biggest!"; Beaver: "again? look who touched the line first"; Mole laughs:
   the same as in the forest.
3. **Interactions, all different**: **child** points to the toy that came first (the ball - small and the fastest;
   the block second, the teddy last); Mole made paper medals - **child** drags each medal to the toy that won it
   (`sort` onto the toys: gold to the ball, silver to the block, bronze to the teddy); Beaver's photos of the forest race
   got mixed up - on a plain colour the **child** puts them in order (`historyjka` wyscig_lesny: the start, the run, the
   finish, the medals: what was first, second, third and last).
4. **The payoff SEEN**: the event illustration `medale_zabawek` - the toys with their paper medals on a podium of
   books on the rug, the friends clapping.
5. **The home task shown in play**: Mole and Beaver race to the window, Owl judges - **child** points to who got
   there first and the winner says "I'm first, and you're second!"; then the line for the parents: race them to the
   door and say who was first, second and last.

The painted room's drop places are not used: the rug is the race track (things lie on it), the windowsill and the
bookcase shelves lie outside the 4:3 safe area or carry no order; the medals go onto the toys.
