# The queue - the great forest race

Goal: ordinal numbers - first, second, third ... last - say place in order, counted from the front (the finish, the
head of a queue), not size. Age 4-5. Characters: Mole, Owl, Beaver. Stage 5, lesson 20.

**Misconception:** the biggest is first - Mole: "Bunny won, he's the biggest!", but the little hedgehog crossed the
line first.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Meadow.* The race: four animals run their lanes to the finish, so close that nobody can tell who was first.
Mole's mistake ("Bunny won, he's the biggest"); Owl: first is who reached the finish first. Mole did not see who that
was; Beaver took a photo of the finish line (`znalezisko` fotofinisz, flies up from Beaver): **child** marks who
touches the line first (the hedgehog - so small, and first; Owl reads the photo: Bunny second, Duckling third), then
who is last (the mouse far behind). Beaver: the medals are given on the podium; while it gets ready, the playground.
B. *Playground.* A queue for the slide, counted from the arrow at the front: **child** points to the third; the first
slides down and joins the end - who is first now? *Breather:* Mole and Owl race to the swings, Beaver judges - who got
there first (the one who really arrived first, whoever started first). Beaver: the podium is ready.
C. *The podium glade* (new scenery `podium_wyscigu`: bunting and flowers; the podium is a painted sticker). Arrival,
a look around. **Child** puts the first three on the podium (1 in the middle and highest, 2 left, 3 right; `slots`
with numbers). Owl names the medals: gold for first, silver for second, bronze for third; the gold flies to Hedgehog;
**child** points to who gets the silver (the second); the bronze goes to Duckling. Confetti; Mole: it was not the
biggest who won, but the fastest.

## Stage 2 - practice
The second in a queue; the last; a race and its podium (the same podium sticker); which medal is for second place (gold, silver, bronze); the
bronze medal for the third in the queue.

## Stage 3 - at home (room)
The toys queue for bed in the order Mole says (teddy, ball, block into numbered places). "At dinner tell who sat down
first, second and last."
