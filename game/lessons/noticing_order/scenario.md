# Noticing order - Owl's picture book

Goal: things happen in order - first, then, last; a story told from its pictures; a missing step. Age 3-4. Characters:
Owl, Bunny, Fox. Stage 3, lesson 9.

**Misconception:** any order will do - Bunny puts the pages back anyhow ("the order doesn't matter"): the hen first,
then the egg, the chick last. At home it comes back as "everything at once, any order": Bunny stacks a sandwich from the
top down.

**One thread:** Owl's new picture book (egg, chick, hen). Bunny asks whether a chick really comes out of an egg; they go
to see it in the henhouse behind Fox's garden and see it hatch. Owl promises: first home, then a snack, last the whole
book read aloud - the home stage keeps that promise.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Forest.* Owl's new picture book; Bunny grabs it in a hurry - the pages (the painted strip `jajko_kura`) fly out and
land scattered and turned. Bunny puts them back anyhow, "the order doesn't matter": hen, egg, chick. Owl reads it and it
is nonsense - they both laugh; Owl: a story happens in order - first, then, last. **Child** puts the three pages in order
(an arrow on the first place); Owl reads it again, each page bumping as it is named. Bunny: but is it true, does a
chick come out of an egg? Owl: let's go to Fox - behind his garden there is a henhouse.
B. *Garden.* Fox is planting a seed. The method on a plain-colour **insert**: the seed grows in one place - seed,
sprout, flower - while Owl says "first, then, last". Back in the garden Fox's pictures of it are mixed - **child** orders
them; Fox names them. *Helping:* Fox's snowman story is missing its middle page - **child** finds it among pages from
other stories. *Breather* (`lustro`): the snowman's merry face gives Bunny an idea - he pulls faces, **child** points to
the same face. A cluck from behind the fence: the hen is calling, off to the henhouse.
C. *The henhouse yard* (`kurnik`, the dedicated last place: a red henhouse, a wicker fence, sunflowers). Bunny looks
round ("a red little house - does the hen live here?"); Fox shows the egg in the nest; it wobbles. **Child** points to
what comes next (the chick, not the hen - the hen is last). The payoff SEEN: the event illustration `wyklucie` - the
chick hatches, the hen bends over it, the friends watch. Bunny gets it: everything happens in order, like in the book.
Owl: first home, then a snack, and last I'll read you the whole book.

## Stage 2 - practice
A cake in order; what came first (the egg); the snowman in order; what comes last (the flower); memory of story
pictures. Each right answer named ("Yes - first flour and eggs, then we stir, last the cake."), the memory pairs named too.
Five stars.

## Stage 3 - at home: the third act (`dom`, the kitchen)
1. **Arrival with the thread**: home, in order - first the snack, then the book.
2. **The misconception again**: Bunny makes the sandwiches "all at once, any order": the cheese on the plate, the
   butter on the cheese, the bread on top - the stack wobbles, the bread slides off onto the floor (`pot`). The method
   on an **insert**: the three sandwich pictures one after another - first bread, then butter, last cheese.
3. **Interactions, all different**: **child** points to what goes on the plate first (the bread); **child** puts the
   sandwich steps in order on the painted kitchen counter (`miejsca` blat, three places, an arrow at the first); after
   eating **child** puts the plate away on the painted shelf (`miejsca` polka_dolna) - first we eat, then we tidy up.
4. **The payoff SEEN**: the event illustration `czytanie_ksiazeczki` - Owl reads the whole book at the kitchen table.
5. **The home task shown in play**: three pictures of today lie on the counter (the book, the flower, the chick);
   **child** points to them in order and Bunny tells each part of the day. Owl: "Tell your parents a story in three
   pictures."

## Iterations
- **v-next, 2026-10-29** (lesson pass): the story no longer ends in the shared garden - it ends in a place of its own,
  the henhouse yard (`kurnik`), where the book's story is SEEN for real (`wyklucie`), which is also why they go there
  (Bunny's doubt); the hop race (first / last - maths, not a breather) is gone, the breather is `lustro` (Bunny pulls
  faces, sparked by the snowman's face); the scattered pages are the painted strip `jajko_kura`, Bunny's "the order
  doesn't matter" is said out loud; the seed's growth is the method on a plain-colour insert; the three bare "Brawo,
  wszystko po kolei!" in practice became lines that name the answer (the memory too); "... Wskaż." lines made
  one sentence; "five games" dropped (no counting past three before the counting lessons). The home stage is a third
  act: the promised snack and book, Bunny's upside-down sandwich, the method insert, the bread first, the counter in
  order, the plate on the shelf, the reading SEEN, the day told in three pictures. Editing pass: dialogues as two-shots,
  no wide shot cut away at once, instructions in the task's shot, close-ups only for reactions.
