# Noticing order - Owl's picture book

Goal: things happen in order - first, then, last; a story told from its pictures; a missing step. Age 3-4. Characters:
Owl, Bunny, Fox. Stage 3, lesson 9.

**Misconception:** any order will do - Bunny puts the pages back anyhow: the hen first, then the egg, the chick last.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Forest.* Owl's new picture book; Bunny grabs it in a hurry - the pages fly out. Bunny puts them back anyhow; Owl
reads it and it is nonsense - everybody laughs. A story happens in order: first, then, last. **Child** puts the three
pages in order (an arrow on the first place); Owl reads it again, each page bumping as it is named.
B. *Garden.* Fox plants a seed and they watch it grow - seed, sprout, flower, said "first, then, last"; the pictures
mixed - **child** orders them. *Helping:* Fox's snowman story is missing its middle page - **child** finds it among
pages from other stories. *Breather:* a race to the tree - who was first, who was last.

## Stage 2 - practice
A cake in order; what came first (the egg); the snowman in order; what comes last; memory of story pictures.

## Stage 3 - at home
A sandwich: bread, butter, cheese in order. "Tell your parents a story in three pictures."
