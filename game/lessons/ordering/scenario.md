# In order - Beaver's evening concert

Goal: put things in order by size, from the biggest to the smallest and back, and see why order matters. Age 3-4.
Characters: Beaver, Bunny, Owl (and her owlets). Stage 2, lesson 6.

## Why rethought (three times)
The first lesson was three unconnected rows; the second had one good consequence (a tower stands only in order) but the
practice and the home stage were the same drag-into-places game, "without flair". Then ONE thread ran through all three
stages - tonight Beaver's forest concert - and order mattered in three ways: a tower stands, a path crosses, a tune
sounds right. The audit (2026-10-29): the tower falls (good), but the concert promised all lesson was never SEEN. Now the
home act ends with the concert itself.

**Misconception:** "a row is a row" - Bunny piles blocks anyhow (the tower falls), swaps two owlets for a joke (the choir
sings out of tune) and, at home, plays the glasses just as they stand (the tune is a mess).

## Stage 1 - story
A. **The brook.** Beaver: a concert tonight, first a tower for a lantern so the guests find the way. Bunny piles a big
block on a small one - it tips and falls. "In order - how?" The method on a plain-colour **insert**: the four blocks
side by side, each named as it bumps - the biggest, a smaller one, smaller still, the smallest. Back at the brook the
**child** builds the tower (`wieza`). The lantern is an empty jar: "the fireflies will light it" - **child** joins the
fireflies from the smallest to the biggest (`kropki` latarenka_swietlikow, `wielkosc`), the glowing lantern appears, goes
up on the tower and shines (`rozblysk`).
B. **The map** (`znalezisko`): Beaver's old map of the way to the glade; **child** marks where the dotted path crosses
the brook. Owl's owlets wait on the far bank and cannot fly yet. **Child** lays stepping stones into the water from the
lowest (by the owlets) to the highest; the owlets hop across one after another and grow as they come near.
C. **The concert glade** (`polana_koncert`). The choir rehearsal: **child** lines the owlets up from the smallest; they
sing (`chor_sowiatek`). Bunny swaps two - the song goes sour (`chor_falsz`); **child** finds an owlet out of its place;
back in order it sounds right. The sun goes down: **breather** - Owl asks the child to paint light on the evening sky
(`malowanie`, `tlo: "noc"`), "so the night is bright for the concert". Beaver: home now, to get his instrument ready.

## Stage 2 - practice
Cups from the smallest; carrots from the biggest; the apple that fits the gap; which row of leaves is in order; the
story strip of the lantern tower in four pictures (`historyjka` wieza_latarenki) - first the big red block, the lantern
last. Each right answer is named ("Yes, the cups from the smallest to the biggest!").

## Stage 3 - at home: the third act (`pokoj_noc`, evening)
1. **Arrival with the thread**: in Beaver's room in the evening - the concert is soon, and his instrument is here: four
   water glasses on the floor.
2. **Listen**: **child** points to each glass and hears each is different.
3. **The misconception again**: Bunny - "a row is a row!" - plays them just as they stand: a mess of notes. Beaver
   sweats: glasses must stand in order too.
4. **The method on an insert**: two glasses alone, the full one and the nearly empty one - much water sounds low, little
   water high. **Child** points to the glass that sounds the lowest (the fullest).
5. **Order on the painted rug** (`c.miejsca()` dywan: four places along the rug): **child** puts the glasses from the
   fullest; they play a rising scale; a pencil line over the water tops goes down like steps while the notes go up.
6. **Child plays it**, glass after glass in order.
7. **The payoff SEEN**: "Time for the concert!" - the event illustration `koncert_noc`: the night glade, the lanterns,
   the tower with its lantern, the owlet choir in order, Beaver on the glasses, Bunny on a drum, Owl conducting.
8. **The home task**: "With your parents, pour water into a few glasses, put them in order and listen to them play" -
   shown just before by Beaver and the child on screen.
