# In order (seriation)

Goal: three to five things in order by size or length into numbered places.
Characters: Deer (left), Fox (right).

## Stage 1 - story - kitchen, garden, children's room

1. Kitchen, the problem: three cups to put away one inside another; Fox tries the big
   one into the small one (it bounces off). Deer ("?"): it will not fit - put them in
   order, from the smallest to the biggest (Deer pictures the row). The child drags
   the cups into places 1, 2, 3. Solved: the small one goes into the middle one, the
   middle one into the big one.
2. Garden: four carrots from the shortest to the longest.
3. Room: a tower of blocks - the biggest at the bottom (places from the bottom up).

## Stage 2 - practice - playground

Five rows: balls, apples (from the smallest), leaves, cones (from the biggest), five
pears.

## Stage 3 - at home - hallway

The family's shoes (from the smallest), socks (from the biggest), teddies. "Ask your
parents to line up the whole family's shoes with you."
