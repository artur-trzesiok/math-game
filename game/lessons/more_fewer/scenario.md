# Lesson 2: More, fewer (and the signs =, <, >)

Goal: the child decides which of two piles has more by pairing one to one and
seeing what is left over; then reads =, < and > as "the hungry mouth opens towards
the pile with more". No counting at any point. Characters: Fox (left) and Owl (right).

## Stage 1 - story (picnic by the lake)

Six choices in the story. 1. Fox brought three cups, Owl two plates. Action: the
   wind rolls a cup down to the water; the child spots the runaway, Fox runs after
   it and brings it back. "Will every cup get a plate?" The rows
   slide into pairs; the cup without a pair pulses and the child points to it. Fox:
   more cups than plates; Owl: fewer plates, one is missing. Bridge: spoons at the table.
2. The sign between the piles: the child picks ">" (mouth towards the cups) from two
   cards. Fox adds a plate: the child picks "=" from three. Owl takes two cups away:
   the child picks "<". Finally the child points to the pile with more. Summary.

## Stage 2 - practice

- Four tasks: two piles of one object (1-4 each); "where is more? point to it". The
  piles are point to targets (outlined zones). A wrong pick triggers the pairing
  animation as the explanation, then the question again.
- Four tasks: two piles and three sign cards (<, =, >) below; "which sign fits?".
  Two of the four are equal. The chosen sign slides between the piles.

## Stage 3 - apply (kitchen)

Owl and Fox set the table on screen five times (2/3, 3/2, 3/3, 1/3, 4/4 cups and
plates): each round is paired, then the child picks the sign or points to the
pile with more. They end by asking the child to ask the parents to check the
table in pairs at home. No card, no text.

## Notes

The signs are drawn as big black glyphs on white cards (engine `sign`), never
named "less-than"; in speech they are "the mouth" and "the equal lines".
