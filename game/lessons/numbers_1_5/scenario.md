# Numbers 1 to 5 - Mole's train

Goal: count to five, every thing once; the digits 4 and 5 (1-3 are known from stage 3); a number says how many to give,
not only how many there are; five spread out is still five. Age 4-5. Characters: Mole, Bear, Owl. Stage 4, lesson 13.

**Misconception:** a longer row has more - Bear's five apples lie far apart, Owl's five pears close: "I have more!".
**The thread:** Mole's train goes to the lake; at the lake Mole promises a picnic there tomorrow; at home we pack for it
and the picnic is seen.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Station* (`stacyjka`). Mole's little train goes through the forest to the lake; Bear wants to ride (a two-shot). A
ticket costs as many coins as its number - Bear's costs four, a new digit. **Insert** (plain colour, digits drifting):
1, 2, 3 drawn ("you know these"), then the new 4 and the new 5 ("my train has five wagons"). Back at the station the
**child** pays four coins (`sklepik`, a fifth jumps back) and writes the 4 on Bear's ticket (`slad`).
B. Five wagons numbered 1-5: the **child** points to the wagon of the mouse's ticket (2) and the bunny's (5) - each
right answer named ("Tak, wagon numer dwa!"); Hedgehog knows his three and hops in by himself. The duckling and the
snail run up late: the duckling has a one and hops in; the snail does not know which wagon is four - **we help him**:
the **child** points to wagon four, he thanks. Mole calls the departure.
C. *The ride* (new panorama `kolej_lesna`): the engine (joined from dots in practice) and the five wagons with their
passengers ride along the painted track while the forest slides by (the pan IS the ride) - the birches, the pine
forest, the forest stop. Everyone gets off, the train waits further on. Bear's apples in a long row, Owl's pears
close: "mine are more!" - the **child** counts both (`count`): five and five; Bear gets it (a bulb). Back on the train:
the ride over the high bridge **seen** (`pociag_most`, Owl and Bear speak over it).
D. *The lakeside stop* (`stacja_jezioro`, the end of the journey): Mole calls the end of the trip, Owl looks round; the
five passengers hop off one by one and the **child** counts them - five, everybody got here. *Breather* (`karmienie`,
not maths): a hungry duck on the lake - the **child** drags duck grain to her (corn, oats, peas - never bread), she gulps it and spits out the pine cone.
Mole: we go home now, and tomorrow we come back for a picnic; Bear: then we must pack!

## Stage 2 - practice (an insert, five stars)
How many (4-5 things) - a digit card ("Tak, są cztery!"); a ticket for five coins; write the 5; join the dots 1-5
(`kropki` `lokomotywa_piec`, digits) - Mole's engine appears; which stone is 4 - Mole hops there along the stepping
stones (`os`). Every answer named, a close-up or medium shot on it.

## Stage 3 - at home: the third act (`pokoj`)
1. **Arrival with the thread**: home; tomorrow the picnic at the lake, so we pack. Bear takes apples, Owl sandwiches.
2. **The misconception again**: on an **insert** Bear's five apples lie in a long row, Owl's five sandwiches close -
   "I have more food!"; Owl: a long row again? let's check on a chart.
3. **Interactions, all different**: the **child** drags every apple and sandwich into its column of a picture chart
   (`wykres`) - two columns of one height; the **child** points to the digit of the column (5); Bear: five and five, a
   long row is not more. Back in the room the **child** packs five things from the painted windowsill into the backpack
   on the rug (`miejsca` parapet, dywan; a sixth: "the backpack is full"); five fingers to wave to the ducks - the
   **child** points to the digit 5 (gest_papier).
4. **The payoff SEEN**: the event illustration `piknik_nad_jeziorem` - the next day, the three on a checked blanket by
   the lake, the five packed things laid out, the ducks fed.
5. **The home task shown in play**: five claps, all together, counted out loud; Bear: count to five with your parents -
   stairs, spoons, claps.
