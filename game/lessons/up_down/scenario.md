# Up and down - apple day

Goal: up and down - up is towards the sky, down towards the ground, whichever way you stand; at the top, at the bottom,
higher. Age 3-4. Characters: Squirrel, Fox, Bunny. Stage 4, lesson 14.

**Misconception:** up and down depend on how you stand - Bunny stands on his head: "now the apples are down and the
grass is up!". Fox: up is always the sky, down the grass. At home Bunny does it again with the hooks.

**The thread:** the apples are ripe at the top of Squirrel's tree - "we'll bake an apple pie from them". The story brings
them down and home; the home stage bakes the pie (seen).

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Orchard (`sad`).* The apples are ripe at the very top of Squirrel's tree; we'll bake a pie. Bunny stands on his head:
"now the apples are down and the grass is up!"; everybody laughs. **Insert** on a plain sky colour (Bunny still on his
head, the sun up, a stone down): Fox - up is always where the sky is, down where the grass is, even on your head; Bunny
flips back - "the sky up, the grass down, always". Back in the orchard: how to get the apples down? Fox has drawn a plan
of a lift - a gust tears it out of his paw (leaves blow): **child** puts the torn plan together (`podarty` plan_windy,
4 pieces), then marks what is at the very top, on the branch (the wheel) and what at the very bottom, in the grass (the
basket). Fox builds the lift as the plan says (`winda`). *Breather:* Bunny wants to send the basket up, the child too -
rock, paper, scissors against Bunny decides (`kpn`, taught first). The winner sends it up: the **child** pulls the rope
or drags the basket, or Bunny pulls it himself. Squirrel puts the apples in; **child** brings the basket down to Bunny.
B. *Autumn orchard (`jesienny_sad`, panorama), the wind.* A gust scatters everything: **child** sorts what flies up
(balloon, bird, butterfly) and what falls down (apple, stone, cone). They walk on through the orchard (a pan) to a fork.
Squirrel's map home: her cottage is up on the hill, the other path goes down to the pond in the valley - **child** leads
the basket along the path that goes up (`wedrowka` mapa_wzgorza; the path down shakes it back: "that one goes down to the
pond"). "Up the hill, home!"

## Stage 2 - practice (a plain-colour sky)
The jar on the top shelf, the shoe on the bottom one (`slots`); Teddy rides the lift to the very top and the very bottom;
which bird is higher; a tower - what is at the very top, at the very bottom; sky or ground (sort). Every right answer is
named ("Tak, słoik na górnej półce, but na dolnej"); a close-up only on the praise, a medium shot on the tower before the
question; five stars.

## Stage 3 - at home: the third act (`przedpokoj`, Squirrel's hallway)
1. **Arrival with the thread**: home with the apples - now the pie. But the wind has blown the door open: the hallway is
   a mess (things scattered and turned on the floor).
2. **The misconception again**: Bunny stands on his head - "now the hooks are down, I'll hang the shoe on them!"; Fox in
   a two-shot: the hooks are always up, near the ceiling, the floor always down; Bunny flips back.
3. **Interactions, all different**: **child** hangs the hat, scarf and backpack up on the painted hooks and puts the shoe
   and the ball down on the doormat (`miejsca` wieszak + wycieraczka, a sort); **child** stands the apple basket on the
   very top shelf of the shoe rack, where the ball cannot knock it over (`miejsca` szafka_gora / srodek / dol, slots);
   Bunny lets go of his balloon from the orchard - it floats up under the ceiling: **child** points to what is at the very
   top now (balloon / basket / ball).
4. **The payoff SEEN**: the event illustration `szarlotka_wiewiorki` - the apple pie on the table, the hooks tidy.
5. **The home task shown in play**: Fox's game "jump up!" - one of them claps instead: **child** points to who got it
   wrong (characters as targets); Squirrel: play it with your parents - jump up, hands up to the sky, hands down to the
   floor.
