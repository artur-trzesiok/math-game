# Up and down - apple day

Goal: up and down - up is towards the sky, down towards the ground, whichever way you stand; at the top, at the bottom,
higher. Age 3-4. Characters: Squirrel, Fox, Bunny. Stage 4, lesson 14.

**Misconception:** up and down depend on how you stand - Bunny stands on his head: "now the apples are down and the
grass is up!". Fox: up is always the sky, down the grass.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Orchard.* The apples are ripe at the top of Squirrel's tree. Bunny on his head; everybody laughs; Fox explains.
But how to get the apples down? Fox has drawn a plan of a lift - a found picture (`znalezisko`
plan_windy): **child** marks what is at the very top, on the branch (the wheel the rope goes over) and what at the very
bottom, in the grass (the basket the apples ride down into). Fox builds the lift as the plan says (`winda`): **child** sends the basket up to the apples, Squirrel puts them in, **child** brings it down to Bunny.
B. *Autumn orchard, the wind.* **Child** sorts what flies up (balloon, bird, butterfly) and what falls down (apple,
stone, cone). *Breather:* "jump up!" - one of them claps instead: who got it wrong (characters as targets).

## Stage 2 - practice
The jar on the top shelf, the shoe on the bottom one (`slots`); Teddy rides the lift to the very top and the very
bottom; which bird is higher; a tower - what is at the very top, at the very bottom; sky or ground (sort).

## Stage 3 - at home (hallway)
Hat, scarf and backpack up on the hooks, shoe and ball down on the floor (sort). "Hands up to the sky, hands down to
the floor with your parents."
