# Tangram - Owl's shadow theatre

Goal: a picture from pieces - every piece has its place; a piece that does not fit is turned (a tap turns it 45
degrees), not thrown away; the same pieces make different pictures. Age 4-5. Characters: Owl, Bear, Squirrel.
Stage 4, lesson 16.

**Misconception:** "this piece is wrong" - Bear tries the roof triangle turned the wrong way and throws it away. At home
he does it again with the fox's ear.

**The thread:** a shadow theatre tonight - the puppets are made from shapes. The story ends with the show; Bear "wants
more", so the home stage is a theatre on the bedroom wall, seen at the end.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Room in the evening (`pokoj_noc`).* Owl and Squirrel in a two-shot: a shadow theatre tonight, but no puppets. Owl
makes them from shapes; Bear's roof triangle "does not fit" - he throws it away. **Insert** on a plain colour: the same
triangle alone, Owl taps it twice, its point comes up like a roof; Bear: "it wasn't wrong, only turned". Back in the
room: **child** builds the house (the triangle starts turned); it turns into its shadow and nods.
B. *Theatre (`teatrzyk`).* **Child** builds on the sheet a fish (its shadow swims) and a boat (it rocks). Squirrel finds
the show's poster - torn: Bear's paw caught it. **Child** puts it together (`podarty plakat_teatru`, 4 pieces), then
marks the cat's ears: two small triangles, the head a turned square - pieces we have. **Child** builds the cat, its
shadow nods. *Breather:* before the show the actors practise their faces - Squirrel pulls faces, the **child** finds the
same one (`lustro`, 3 rounds). The show; the audience claps.

## Stage 2 - practice (a plain colour)
A square from two triangles; the cat has lost an ear - which piece fits the gap (the medium triangle: "the right shape,
but too big"; the square: another shape); whose shadow (the hedgehog); a bird's shadow from dots (`kropki cien_ptaka`,
from the smallest); a rocket of two squares and three small triangles - it flies off. Every right answer is named.

## Stage 3 - at home: the third act (`pokoj_noc`, back in the bedroom at night)
1. **Arrival with the thread**: the show is over and Bear wants more - Owl: a shadow theatre here, on the wall; we need
   a light.
2. **Child** puts the lamp on the painted rug, facing the wall (`miejsca` dywan, a sort).
3. **The misconception again**: the puppet is a fox head (a big triangle and two ears); Bear tries an ear turned the
   wrong way and throws it towards the bed; Owl in a two-shot: "again? don't throw it, turn it". **Child** builds the fox;
   its shadow nods on the wall.
4. **The same pieces, another picture**: the fox's three pieces start where the fox was and the **child** builds a tree
   from them.
5. **Bedtime**: the pieces go back into their box (the seven-piece square, all in place but one): **child** points to
   the piece that fits the last gap (the parallelogram; a square and a triangle shake).
6. **The payoff SEEN**: the event illustration `teatr_w_pokoju` - the fox's shadow in the lamp's circle on the wall,
   Bear holding the puppet, Owl and Squirrel under a blanket on the rug.
7. **The home task shown**: an insert - a paper square, cut into two triangles that slide apart; Bear: cut one with your
   parents, make pictures, and a shadow theatre with a torch in the evening.
