# Inside and outside - the evening before the storm

Goal: inside and outside - a closed fence makes an inside, a gap lets things out; next to is not inside. Age 3-4.
Characters: Hedgehog, Beaver, Deer. Stage 4, lesson 15.

**Misconceptions:** a fence with a gap keeps the hens in (Beaver's fence - a hen walks out); next to is inside
(Beaver's duckling "in" the basket stands beside it). At home Beaver does it again with the teddy and the toy box.

**The thread:** "a storm is coming - tonight everybody must be at home". The story gets the hens into their pen and
the duckling into the warm burrow; the home stage is the night after the storm: everybody home, inside, asleep (seen).

## Stage 1 - story
A. *The lake meadow on the evening before the storm (`laka_burza`, a variant of `laka`).* Leaves blow; Hedgehog: a storm
is coming, his hens walk on the meadow and must go back into the pen. Beaver (two-shot): "I'll build them a fence!" - a
fence round the hens with a gap; a hen walks out through it. **Insert** on a plain colour (the method): a ring with a
gap - the hen walks out ("you can get out through a gap"); the ring closed - the hen bumps into it and stays ("closed
all the way round: what is inside stays inside"); Beaver: "a fence with no gap, closed all round". Back on the meadow:
**child** draws a closed fence round the hens (`zagroda`; an open line lets a hen out). Beaver's duckling "in" the basket
stands next to it - Deer wonders, "next to, not inside" - **child** puts it in (`place`).
B. Deer draws a circle on the grass - "everybody inside!"; **child** points to who is still outside (Hedgehog runs in).
The rain starts. **The thunder** (event illustration `grzmot_kaczuszka`): the duckling leaps out of the basket and runs
into the bushes; the basket lies tipped over, empty. The paths between the bushes wind, she will not find the burrow
alone, inside it the rain cannot reach her: **child** leads her along the paths to the burrow door (`labirynt`
sciezki_do_norki).
C. *Hedgehog's burrow in the storm* (`norka_burza`, the dedicated last place). The duckling is inside, dry (a flash);
inside is dry and warm, the rain stayed outside. *Breather:* the warm stove steams up the window - we cannot see outside;
**child** draws on the steamy glass with a finger (`malowanie`, tlo "szyba", the scratch-off pair `szyba_norki`) and the
storm shows through: lightning, rain, bent grass. Deer: outside the storm, and we are inside, in the warm burrow; Beaver:
when the storm is over, home to sleep.

## Stage 2 - practice (a plain colour)
A fence around the sheep, the wolf outside; three apples into the basket; which star is outside the circle; which cup
hides the ball; the goldfish into the bowl. Every right answer is named ("Tak, owieczki są w środku, a wilk został na
zewnątrz"), said with the answer still in the shot; five stars.

## Stage 3 - at home: the third act (`pokoj_noc`, the night after the storm)
1. **Arrival with the thread**: the storm is over, we are home, everybody inside. But the toys lie scattered, the
   duckling's basket on the floor: before bed the toys go back into the box.
2. **The misconception again**: Beaver "helps" - stands the teddy next to the box: "Teddy is in the box already!"; Deer:
   next to the box, not inside; Beaver laughs at himself: "next to is not inside".
3. **Interactions, all different**: **child** drags the toys inside the box and the duckling's basket out onto the
   painted rug (`sort`, the rug a drop place from `miejsca`); the guessing game, the home game shown in play: Hedgehog
   puts the ball out next to the box - **child** points to the toy that is outside; the duckling will not sleep, she
   wants a story of her evening: on a plain night colour (an insert) **child** puts the four pictures of her evening in
   order (`historyjka` wieczor_kaczuszki: in the basket, the thunder, the paths, inside the burrow) and Hedgehog tells it;
   **child** puts the duckling inside her basket on the rug (`place`).
4. **The payoff SEEN**: the event illustration `dobranoc_w_domu` - the duckling asleep in the basket, the toys in the
   box, the friends asleep, the moon outside: everybody inside, at home. Good night.
5. **The home task**: Hedgehog to the parents - hide a toy in a box or put it next to it: inside or outside?

## Iterations
- **v-next, 2026-10-29** (lesson pass): the storm's thread ("tonight everybody must be at home") runs to a home act at
  night where it is seen (`dobranoc_w_domu`). The story happens on a storm-evening meadow (`laka_burza`) instead of the
  sunny one; Deer's explanation of the gap is a plain-colour insert with the hen walking out of an open ring and staying
  in a closed one; the thunder is an event illustration (`grzmot_kaczuszka`) instead of a sticker hop. The breather is
  drawing on the burrow's steamy window (`malowanie` + `szyba_norki`) - the circle game was maths and stays as a story
  task; the story's shell game (a repeat of practice) is cut. Home is a third act: Beaver's "next to is in" again, toys
  into the box / the basket onto the rug, the guessing game shown in play, the duckling's evening as a story strip, the
  duckling into her basket. Practice names every right answer instead of "Brawo!"; the "... Wskaż." lines are one
  sentence; the editing pass: dialogues as two-shots, no wide shot cut away at once, praise said with the answer in the
  shot.
