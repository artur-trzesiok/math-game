# Inside and outside - the evening before the storm

Goal: inside and outside - a closed fence makes an inside, a gap lets things out; next to is not inside. Age 3-4.
Characters: Hedgehog, Beaver, Deer. Stage 4, lesson 15.

**Misconceptions:** a fence with a gap keeps the hens in (Beaver's fence - a hen walks out); next to is inside
(Beaver's duckling "in" the basket stands beside it).

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Meadow.* A storm is coming; Hedgehog's hens must go into the pen. Beaver's fence has a gap, a hen walks out.
Deer: a fence must be closed all the way round. **Child** draws a closed fence around the hens (`zagroda`, new; an
open line: a hen walks out through the gap). Beaver's duckling stands next to the basket - **child** puts it in.
B. *Breather:* Deer draws a circle on the grass - "everybody inside!"; Hedgehog stays out - **child** points to who is
outside. The rain: into Hedgehog's *burrow*; the shell game - which cup is the acorn in (`kubki`).

## Stage 2 - practice
A fence around the sheep, the wolf outside; three apples into the basket; which star is outside the circle; which cup
hides the ball; the goldfish into the bowl.

## Stage 3 - at home (room)
The toys back into the toy box. "A toy into the box, a toy out of the box - inside or outside?"
