# One or many?

Goal: one thing / many things ("jeden" / "wiele"): see at a glance whether there is one of something or many, and
say it. Not yet numbers. Age 3-4. Characters: Squirrel (left), Fox (left), Deer (right). Stage 1, lesson 1.

One thread: **winter is coming and Squirrel gathers her store** - the story fills it (and the first snow nearly
buries the last vegetables), the practice is "checking the store", at home the store goes onto the shelves and
becomes a feast for the friends met on the way (Mother Duck was invited at the brook).

## Stage 1 - story

### A. The garden - big is not many (the concept comes in)
1. Squirrel and Deer in the garden, autumn leaves in the air (two-shot). Squirrel: winter is coming, I need a store -
   lots of food, many nuts! Fox comes in rolling one enormous pumpkin: "I brought you a lot of food!" (the
   misconception: big means many).
2. Deer, the pumpkin in the shot: Fox, that is ONE pumpkin - big, but one. Squirrel (close-up, "?"): then what is
   "many"? **Insert on a plain colour (the method):** many is when there are lots of things - this one, and this,
   and this... (each nut bumps); one pumpkin, many nuts.
3. Back in the garden: **child** points: where is one thing? (the pumpkin) Where are many? (the nuts).
4. The harvest over their heads: one pear, many apples, one mushroom, many acorns, one carrot, many leaves.
   **Child** drags each onto the plate ("one") or into the basket ("many"). Fox laughs at himself: then the
   pumpkin goes to the store too - one big pumpkin and many nuts!
5. *Breather, no maths:* the wind blows harder - Fox's kite. **Child** pulls it up into the sky until it loops.

### B. The brook - helping Mother Duck
6. Mother Duck is upset: her many ducklings came back, one is missing. **Child** finds the one duckling alone by
   the reeds; it swims back; Squirrel invites the duck family to her feast; home, it is getting cold.

### C. The first snow (the story's own place: the snowy garden)
7. **Event illustration:** at night the first snow falls; Squirrel looks out of her hollow: good thing the store is in!
8. Morning, the garden is white (Deer, cold): vegetables were left under the snow. **Child** brushes the snow off
   (scratch-off): one cabbage and many carrots. **Child** points to every carrot - for the feast.

## Stage 2 - practice (an insert, autumn leaves drifting, five stars) - "checking the store"
1. **Listen:** Fox claps once or many times; **child** points to the card - one clap / many claps (`dzwieki`).
2. **Cups:** one acorn under one cup, many under another; the cups mix (`kubki`); where are many?
3. **Memory:** pairs one apple-one apple, many apples-many apples, one nut-one nut, many nuts-many nuts (`memory`).
4. **Shadows:** whose shadow is it - one pear or many pears? (`cien`).
5. **One among many:** many stars and one moon - point to what there is one of.

## Stage 3 - at home - the third act (the kitchen, painted places)
9. Arrival with the thread: home, the guests are coming - first the store onto the shelves: one thing goes on the
   top shelf, many things on the bottom shelf.
10. The home problem with the same misconception: Fox puts his huge pumpkin on the bottom shelf, "where there's a
    lot". Deer: it is huge, but one. **Child** drags the pumpkin up; then the nuts down (`miejsca`: the painted
    shelves). Fox: now I know.
11. **Child** lays the painted table: one cake onto the plate, many cherries into the bowl.
12. Knock, knock - the guests: one hedgehog from one side, many ducklings from the other. **Child** points to who came
    alone.
13. The payoff: **event illustration** - everybody at the feast, snow outside. Fox: look around at home with your
    parents - one table, many spoons!
