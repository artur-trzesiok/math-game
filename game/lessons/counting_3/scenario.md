# Counting to 3 - Hedgehog's birthday

Goal: count to three by touch, each thing once; the last number tells how many; the digits 1, 2, 3. Age 3-4.
Characters: Hedgehog, Bear, Squirrel. Stage 3, lesson 10.

**Misconception:** counting a thing twice - Bear touches the middle candle twice and says "four!".

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Meadow.* Hedgehog is three today. Bear brings candles and counts "one, two, three, four" (the middle one bumps
twice). Squirrel: each thing once - the last number says how many. **Child** counts the candles by touch (`count`).
The digits 1, 2, 3 drawn one after another; **child** traces the 3 (`slad`).
B. *Forest.* Three raspberries for the cake: **child** drags them to Bear one at a time; a fourth jumps back ("I already
have three"). *Breather:* Hedgehog claps for his guests (1-3 claps) - **child** points to the digit card.

## Stage 2 - practice
How many apples (a digit card); give Squirrel two acorns; trace 1 and 2; how many drum beats; which heap has three pears.

## Stage 3 - at home (kitchen)
The cake with three candles - count them; three spoons for three friends. "Count to three with your parents."
