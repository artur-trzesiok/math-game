# Numbers 6 to 10 - Beaver's dam

Goal: count past five to ten, each thing once (a counted thing moves aside into a row); 6-10 as five and some more
(the ten frame); the digits 6-10; ten is written with two digits. Age 5-6. Characters: Beaver, Mole, Owl.
Stage 6, lesson 22.

**Misconception:** losing track in a heap - Mole counts the logs lying anyhow, some twice: "twelve!".

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Brook.* Beaver's dam has a hole; ten logs are needed. Mole counts the heap (logs bump twice) - "twelve!". Owl: put
every counted log aside. **Child** counts the logs into a row (`count` with `rzad`): eight, two missing. Owl's ten
frame (`ramka`, new): **child** drags eight logs in - five and three.
B. *Forest.* **Child** fills the frame up to ten (one more jumps back: "full"). The digits 6, 7, 8, 9 drawn; ten with
two digits. *Breather:* Mole hops along the stones 0-10 to the number Beaver says - **child** points to the stone.

## Stage 2 - practice
How many in the frame (6-10) - a number card; seven apples into the frame; write the 7; which heap has more, eight or
six (the six heap drawn bigger); which stone is 9.

## Stage 3 - at home (room)
Two hands - how many fingers (ten, as the frame). "Count ten toys into the box with your parents."
