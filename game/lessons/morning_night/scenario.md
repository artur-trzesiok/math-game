# Morning and night - Mole's long day

Goal: the parts of the day in order - morning, midday, evening, night - and what we do in each; before / after.
Age 3-4. Characters: Mole (new), Deer, Squirrel. Stage 2, lesson 8.

**Misconception:** Mole lives underground and sees badly - he thinks it is always night.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Burrow* (the new scenery, dim). Mole wakes: is it morning? Underground it's always dark. Squirrel and Deer come down
the tunnel: it's morning, come up - we'll show you a whole day.
B. *Meadow.* The dark lifts as the sun rises (`noc` fading out). Morning: **child** points to what we do then
(breakfast, among the bed and pyjamas). The day on four cards - **child** puts them in order (morning, midday, evening,
night; an arrow marks the first place).
C. Evening falls (`noc`). *Helping:* little Hedgehog is lost in the bushes - **child** shines a light on a bush
(`swiatlo`); an empty one stays lit. Night: catch three fireflies. Mole: after the night comes the morning again.

## Stage 2 - practice
What comes after evening; what we do at night; three cards in order; memory of day cards; what comes after night.

## Stage 3 - at home (the room at night)
The evening routine in order: bath, pyjamas, a story, bed. "Tell your parents what happened this morning, at midday and
this evening."
