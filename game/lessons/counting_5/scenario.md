# Counting to 5 - the forest bakery

Goal: count to five - one number for each thing, the last number is how many; moving things does not change how many
(conservation); five shown as a row, dots (a domino) and a hand. Age 4-5. Characters: Bear, Squirrel, Hedgehog.
Stage 6, lesson 21.

**Misconception:** spread out = more - Bear spreads the five rolls along the counter: "now there are more!".

## Stage 1 - story
A. *Bakery* (new scenery). **Child** counts five rolls on the tray (`count`). Bear spreads them out - "more!";
Hedgehog: did any appear? **Child** counts again - five. The first order: **child** packs four rolls into a bag (a
fifth jumps back).
B. *Orchard.* Which heap has five apples; which domino has five dots (`domino`, new: 2 + 3); *breather:* Hedgehog's
jumps counted by ear (3-5) - a digit card. Back at the bakery the first customer pays five coins (`sklepik`).

## Stage 2 - practice
How many rolls (3-5) - a digit card; three rolls into the bag; which tray has five (five spread vs four close); memory of
numbers and dots; the fourth roll from the arrow.

## Stage 3 - at home (kitchen)
Five guests: count five plates, then five cups - one for each. "Count five stairs with your parents."
