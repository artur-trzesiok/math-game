# Counting to 5 - the forest bakery

Goal: count to five - one number for each thing, the last number is how many; moving things does not change how many
(conservation); five shown as a row, as dots (memory, domino). Age 4-5. Characters: Bear, Squirrel, Hedgehog; Owl at the
end of the story. Stage 6, the only number lesson of the stage and the last of world 1: nothing past five, no adding.

**Misconception:** spread out = more - Bear spreads the five rolls along the counter: "now there are more!"; at home he
thinks the plates spread over the table are more than the cups crowded on the counter.

**Thread:** the bakery's first day. Owl's order is delivered to her tree; Bear invites Owl and her owlets to the opening
supper at home; at home the table is set for the five guests and the supper is seen.

## Stage 1 - story
A. *The bakery* (piekarnia). Bear opens his forest bakery - the first rolls are baked. He wants to dust them with flour:
the sack tips over, everything is white (`zdrapka maka_na_ladzie`): the **child** wipes the flour off - the tray is
there - and **counts** the rolls under it: five, the last number says how many. The five rolls on the tray; Bear
spreads them along the counter "to look nice": "now there are more!". Hedgehog wonders; on a plain-colour insert the
rolls lie spread and the **child counts** again: still five. The rolls glide back together: five is five, close or far
- Bear gets it. Back in the bakery a note under the door with a feather - Owl's order (`zamowienie_sowy`): the **child
counts** the rolls she drew - four. Bear packs four into a bag, counting aloud; Squirrel: let's take them to Owl, she
lives past the orchard.
B. *The orchard* (sad), on the way. *Breather* (`karmienie`): a little bird chirps, hungry - the **child** feeds it the
bird seed (never bread) and leaves it the pine cone. "Owl is waiting" - on we go.
C. *Owl's tree* (`dziupla_sowy`, new, the story's own last place). The wide hollow high in the beech; Bear calls; Owl
comes out: the rolls are for her owlets. Four owlets fly down - the **child gives** each one roll (`rozdaj`, one each):
four owlets, four rolls. Owl pays one coin for each roll, but her eyes are sleepy by day - the **child counts out** four
coins onto the plate (`sklepik`, the tag shows four dots). Bear's first payment! He invites Owl and the owlets to the
opening supper at his home tonight; "We will all come!"

## Stage 2 - practice (plain colour)
How many rolls (3-5) - a digit card, the answer named; the domino with five dots (2|3 among 1|2 and 3|1, nothing past
five); which tray has five cookies (five spread vs four close - the long row is not more); memory of digits and dots;
the fourth roll from the arrow. Every right answer is named ("Tak, cztery bułeczki!"), the memory gets `brawo`.

## Stage 3 - at home: the opening supper (kitchen `dom`)
1. Arrival with the thread: the bakery is open, Owl and the owlets are coming tonight; Hedgehog: five guests - let's set
   the table.
2. **Child puts** the plates on the painted table (`miejsca().stol`, five places spread along it).
3. The misconception again: Bear crowds the five cups together on the counter - "there are more plates than cups, not
   enough cups!". Squirrel: let's check - the **child counts** the cups (five), then the **plates** (five): as many.
4. The method on a plain-colour insert: the plates in a long row, the cups crowded; the **child puts** one cup on each
   plate - none is missing, none left over. Bear: enough for everyone!
5. Back in the kitchen, the cups on the plates. Hooting at the door - the **child counts** the hoots by ear and points
   to the digit: five - each guest hooted once, five guests.
6. The payoff seen: the supper (`kolacja_sowiatek`): Owl and the four owlets at the table, a plate and a cup each,
   Bear brings the rolls.
7. The home task shown in play: Bear counts the plates on the table aloud, one by one; then the line for the parents:
   setting the table at home, count the plates and the cups together.
