# Spot the difference - the magic pond

Goal: look carefully at two pictures and find what is not the same (something missing, another colour, another
thing). Age 3-4. Characters: Fox (left), Owl (right). Stage 1, lesson 2.

**Misconception:** a quick look - "it's the same!".

## Stage 1 - story (the meadow)
A. Owl shows Fox the magic pond; Fox looks in (`odbicie`: his reflection in an oval pond, moving with him): "the same
as me!" Owl: at a glance yes, but the pond plays tricks - look carefully. **Child** finds the difference (no scarf in
the water), circled on both. "A difference: something not the same in two pictures." Again with a hat and glasses -
two differences.
B. The pond's game: two pictures of the meadow, three differences (`roznice`). Helping: Owl's drawing for her granny
and the pond's copy - **child** finds the differences, then points to the real one (the kitten, the sun's rays).
Breather: catch three butterflies.

## Stage 2 - practice (an insert, five stars)
Three picture pairs (garden, beach, room); what has gone from a row of four (eyes closed); which of four shadows is
not like the others.

## Stage 3 - at home (the room)
Toys on the rug; eyes closed; two swap places - point to one of them; twice. "Play it at home: one closes their
eyes, the other moves one thing."
