# Spot the difference - the magic pond

Goal: look carefully at two pictures and find what is not the same (something missing, another colour, another
thing). Age 3-4. Characters: Fox (left), Owl (right). Stage 1, lesson 2.

**Misconception:** a quick look - "it's the same!".

One thread: **Owl's drawing for her granny** - the pond copies it, the child finds which one is hers, the friends
carry it to Granny's oak and leave it at her door (by day she sleeps); at home, at night, Granny finds it - and in the
morning the room shows she came to say thank you.

## Stage 1 - story

### A. The magic pond (the meadow)
1. Owl shows Fox the magic pond; Fox looks in (`odbicie`: his reflection in an oval pond, moving with him): "the same
   as me!" (the misconception: a quick look). Owl (close-up): at a glance yes, but the pond plays tricks - look
   carefully. **Child** points to the difference (no scarf in the water), circled on both. Owl names it: a difference
   is something two pictures do not agree in.
2. Again with a hat and glasses - **child** finds both differences.

### B. The pond's pictures (an insert: the pond shows pictures, the meadow fades)
3. **Insert on a plain colour (the method):** the pond shows two pictures of the meadow; Owl: look at one, then at
   the same place on the other. **Child** finds three differences (`roznice`).
4. Helping Owl: she drew a picture for her granny and the pond made a copy - now she has two and does not know which
   is hers. **Child** finds the differences, then points to the real one (the kitten, the sun's rays).
5. *Breather, no maths:* back at the pond, its fish swim up hungry - a thank-you for the pond. **Child** feeds the
   fish with fish food (`karmienie` - never bread, it harms fish); a pine cone it spits back.

### C. The walk to Granny's oak (the panorama `droga_do_debu`)
6. Off along the path through the meadow (a pan); the brook has no stones - **child** lays three stepping stones
   into the water; Fox and Owl hop across; on along the path to the big oak on the hill.
7. *Granny Owl's old oak* (`dab_babci`, a place of its own: a round door high in the trunk). Fox wants to knock -
   "shh, by day Granny sleeps, owls are awake at night"; the drawing flies up and stays by her door. Owl: tonight she
   will find it. Home, before it gets dark.

## Stage 2 - practice (an insert, five stars)
Three picture pairs (garden, beach, room); what has gone from a row (eyes closed, three rounds); which of four shadows
is not like the others.

## Stage 3 - at home - the third act (Fox's room: evening, night, morning)
8. Arrival with the thread (`pokoj_noc`, the evening): Owl sleeps over at Fox's. Owl: tonight Granny finds the
   drawing - and she surely flies over to say thank you, owls fly at night. Fox: then let's put everything in its
   place and remember it - in the morning we will see if anything changed.
9. **Child** puts the toys on the painted rug (`miejsca`), side by side (the rug is the place the evening and the
   morning room share). Owl: look carefully and remember. Good night - the lamp.
10. The payoff, **event illustration**: at night Granny Owl opens her little door and finds the drawing; she presses it
    to her heart (Owl speaks from her badge).
11. The morning (`pokoj`). The home problem with the misconception again: Fox, with one quick look: "everything is the
    same, Granny did not come". Owl: at a glance - like at the pond! **Child** points to the two toys that changed
    places (both; Granny plays tricks like the pond).
12. **Child** points to what was not here last night: a grey feather on the rug - Granny's! She was here and said
    thank you. (The promise kept and seen.)
13. The home task shown in play: Fox: let's play it like Granny - Owl closes her eyes, Fox moves one toy, Owl finds
    it; then the **child** closes their eyes, the ball rolls off the rug, the child points to it. Owl: play it at home - one closes
    their eyes, the other moves one thing.
