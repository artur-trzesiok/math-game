# The programme: worlds -> stages -> lessons (prototype, 2026-10-29)

Four worlds of six stages of four lessons: 96 lessons (the target was ~100). World 1 is the game as it was built
(stages 1-6; on 2026-10-30 stage 6 was reordered, see below - lesson ids and stickers stay valid). Worlds 2-4 are new: 72 lessons, planned from the
competitor's next lessons (`docs/konkurencja.md`) - kept where good, merged where they repeat, moved where our children
are ready earlier or later, and our own added. Stage ids continue: etap7 .. etap24. Every lesson keeps the house rules
(`docs/guidelines.md`): three stages, a story that leads, the third act at home, a breather, one idea.

| world | name | age | stages |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leśna Kraina (the forest) | 3-4 | 1-6 (today's game) |

## What we take, change and add (summary)
- Taken (with our story and look): weight, thickness and width (one lesson), counting to 10, ordinals, tables, equal sets, make equal,
  sorting without a rule, two features, plane shapes II, Schulte (as a hunt), variation rules, sort by quantity, codes,
  decomposition, subtraction, sudoku, solids, symmetry, rotation, clock, even / odd, fair share, 2D ordinals, barter,
  groups, multiview, charts, label classification, facts to 10, tangram II, the arithmetic block.
- Merged / dropped: shape puzzle + complete the shape -> one lesson; "Rules II" -> our rhythm hunt; "Sequential order"
  dropped (we already have noticing_order, ordering and morning_night); the make-ten variants compressed.
- Checked 2026-10-30 (after the stage 6 reorder): ten pairs were taught three times (split_10, bonds_10, make_ten) -
  bonds_10 folded into make_ten, doubles in the freed place; the clock needed 11 and 12 before counting to 20 -> stage 23;
  coins needed adding to 10 -> stages 17 and 18 swapped, change joins coins; two-digit sums had no place value ->
  numbers_100, subtract_three folded into add_sub_three; days of the week before the tables that use them.
- Moved: adding to 5 and to 10 later than in our first build (2026-10-30); measuring with a unit and number neighbours earlier (world 2); symmetry earlier; related add / subtract much
  earlier (world 3, at 5); make-ten pairs before carrying.
- Ours: doubles, numbers to 100, subitizing, capacity, completing to five, shapes hunt, days of the week, guess who, number patterns, seasons, coins, counting to 20, teen numbers, two
  strategies, change, estimation, coding, the finale.
